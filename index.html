Project Overview

The UA EcoCAR team is developing the DriveAbility website in collaboration with UA's Adapted Athletics Department to compete in the National EcoCAR Advanced Vehicle Technologies Competition. The DriveAbility Website is intended to be a trusted resource for individuals with mobility disabilities to identify adaptive vehicle modifications that suit their needs. The website will offer a comprehensive database that organizes both quantitative and qualitative characteristics of various hand-control models, allowing users to easily review key features, specifications, and compatibility across different accessibility products.

Our approach combines secure web design principles with an intutive, accessible website experience.

Project Goals

  • Utility: Develop creative solutions that inform the adaptive automotive community about different products and features
  • Security: Maintain high standards throughout development with rigorous testing and review processes
  • Accessibility: Deploy an easy, intuitive website experience that meets the needs and of all users
